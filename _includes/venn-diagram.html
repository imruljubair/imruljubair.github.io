{%- comment -%} Liquid's `sort` filter isn't guaranteed stable, so `sort: "year" | reverse` can silently reorder same-year items instead of keeping their CSV order. Grouping by year first sidesteps that: group_by preserves each item's original relative order within its group, and the groups themselves (one per distinct year) have no ties to worry about when sorted. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign works = site.data.works -%} {%- assign ai_only = works | where: "type", "ai" | group_by: "year" | sort: "name" | reverse -%} {%- assign viz_only = works | where: "type", "viz" | group_by: "year" | sort: "name" | reverse -%} {%- assign ai_hci = works | where: "type", "ai+hci" | group_by: "year" | sort: "name" | reverse -%} {%- assign triple = works | where: "type", "ai+viz+hci" | group_by: "year" | sort: "name" | reverse -%} {%- comment -%} The diagram sits between two groups on each side on wide screens. On narrow screens it moves above a 2x2 group grid. Both layouts use leader lines. The lines are drawn into .venn-links by the script at the end of this file, from the measured positions of the dots and the list headings. Nothing about them is hard-coded, so they stay attached however long the lists grow or however the text reflows — earlier versions used fixed endpoints and drifted out of alignment every time the data changed. Dot positions are in the SVG's 320x200 viewBox, each inside its own region only, and ordered top-to-bottom to match the list order so no lines cross: AI (196, 52) Viz ( 95, 88) AI + HCI (198, 95) all three (158,115) {%- endcomment -%}
